# 🐍 Jungle Snake Chase

> **An immersive 3D serpentine predation simulator and educational biome crawler.**
> Playable directly in your browser on PC, mobile, and tablets.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://arnavgmallan.github.io/jungle-snake-chase/](https://arnavgmallan.github.io/jungle-snake-chase/)

---

## 🌿 Overview

*Jungle Snake Chase* puts you in control of four legendary ophiophagous (snake-eating) apex serpents across four meticulously simulated biomes. Hunt prey serpents, track scent trails with your forked tongue, stalk hopping critters, and experience continuous kinematics with realistic undulation and digestion physics.

---

## 🗺️ Biomes & Apex Predators

| Biome | Apex Serpent | Prey Serpent | Unique Environmental Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tropical Rainforest** | **King Cobra** (*Ophiophagus hannah*) | Banded Krait (*Bungarus fasciatus*) | Continuous 3D tropical rainfall, lush palms, crocodile-mouth wide swamps, and sluggish nocturnal prey behavior in morning light |
| **Great Big Swamp** | **King Rat Snake** (*Elaphe carinata*) | Bamboo Pit Viper (*Trimeresurus stejnegeri*) | Vast 96m × 96m wetland expanse with a small central sanctuary island, reeds, water lilies, and cypress trees |
| **Arid Desert Dunes** | **King Brown Snake / Mulga** (*Pseudechis australis*) | Young Inland Taipan (*Oxyuranus microlepidotus*) | Red desert sands, natural sandstone arches, and authentic 3D desert flora: tall Saguaros with branched arms, Golden Barrel cacti with yellow blooms, and Prickly Pears (zero water) |
| **Verdant Meadow & Forest** | **California Kingsnake** (*Lampropeltis californiae*) | Western Diamondback Rattlesnake (*Crotalus atrox*) | Pure emerald meadows, woodland canopy, and rattlesnake defensive warning buzz (zero water) |

## 🎮 Features

- **Realistic Serpentine Kinematics**: Multi-vertebrae inverse kinematics, lateral undulation, procedural 3D scales, hoods, realistic eyes, and traveling digestion boluses after feeding.
- **Predator-Prey Ecosystem**:
  - Prey serpents stalk, strike, and swallow hopping frogs/toads across the biomes.
  - Feeding grants growth and dynamic digestion boluses to both player and prey.
- **Synthesized Procedural Audio (Web Audio API)**:
  - High-pressure hissing, tongue flicks, tail rattles, frog croaks, slither friction, and major-chord celebration fanfare—100% synthesized in real time with zero external audio assets.
- **Ages 5 to 83 / Relaxed Tablet Mode**:
  - **Dual-Thumb Ergonomics**: Left thumb tactile D-pad or direct screen touch-to-steer; right thumb large tactile action buttons (`⚡ STRIKE LUNGE` and `👅 Sniff Scent`).
  - **Smart Strike Magnetism**: Tapping Strike when within 7.5m gently snaps onto target for effortless catching.
  - **Generous Strike Reach**: 4.8m normal / 6.4m lunge dash radius.
  - **Visual Strike Range Pulse**: Scent radar banner and strike button pulse gold when prey is in range.
  - **One-Tap Fullscreen (`⛶`)**: Expands to full screen to hide browser toolbars on tablets.
- **100% Self-Contained**:
  - Single-file pure HTML5, WebGL 1.0, and Web Audio architecture.
  - Zero external dependencies, libraries, or CDN assets.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Steering**:
  - **Touch/Tablet**: Left-thumb D-pad or tap/drag anywhere on screen.
  - **Keyboard**: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or Arrow Keys (`A`/`D` turns, `W` speeds up, `S` slows down for stalking).
- **Actions**:
  - **Strike Lunge**: `Spacebar`, `Shift`, or tap `⚡ STRIKE LUNGE`.
  - **Scent Flick**: `T` or tap `👅 Sniff Scent`.
- **Display**:
  - **Fullscreen**: Click `⛶` in the top header.
  - **Audio Toggle**: Click `🔊` in the top header.
  - **Mode Toggle**: Click `🌟 Relaxed Tablet Mode` to switch between Relaxed and Brisk Challenge play.
